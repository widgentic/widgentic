The authoring guide's theme section documents a theme's shared `styles` and that default themes are a setting chosen in Settings, in step with the MCP server's guide. `theme_get` and `theme_load` describe the entry's `styles`, and `theme_load` warns that it replaces the whole entry. The styles rules in the guide derive from the same constants as the MCP guide's.

The settings designer gains a **Stylesheets** subsection.
- **Entries.** An address and an optional integrity value per entry, checked live by the core resource rule, with adding disabled at its limit.
- **Marks.** A stylesheet whose host none of the listed resource domains covers is marked with the domain to add; the mark clears once the domain is listed.
- **The note.** It names the fonts domain (`fonts.gstatic.com` for Google Fonts) and the trust a stylesheet takes.

The widget and theme designers take a `resources` option: the preview island links the person's stylesheets under its frame policy, and the in-page preview ignores them.
